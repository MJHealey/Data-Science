# Wildlife Corridors
**Is the U.S. Government Spending its $350 Million in Wildlife Corridor Funding Wisely?**
`UC Berkeley · Dec 2023`

---

## Overview

A mixed-methods research study designed to analyze how the length and width of wildlife 
corridors impact species crossing rates across 1,200+ corridors in the U.S., Canada, 
Brazil, and the Netherlands.

## Project Summary

- Collaborated on study design, research question framing, and hypothesis development
- Proposed separate linear regression models for predator and prey species
- Developed data collection framework combining public databases, surveys, and 
  camera trap monitoring
- Addressed real-world research challenges including geographic bias, confounding 
  environmental variables, and regulatory compliance (IACUC, state video laws)

*Research design project — no code.*
